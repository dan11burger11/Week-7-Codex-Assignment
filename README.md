The results from my analysis on the impact of 0.5 bathroom incremental increases shows that the impact on sales price varies depending on which tier(1 - 1.5, 2 - 2.5, etc.) the sales  price belongs. Firstly, we should ignore any houses with more than 3.5 baths for this study as the lack of sample size prevents us from being to find anything statistically significant. The highest increase in mean sales price for the 0.5 bathroom increase was from 2.0 to 2.5 bathrooms with a 27.8% increase in mean sales price. Its also interesting to note that the .5 to .0 increases (1.5 to 2.0 & 2.5 to 3.0 bathrooms) had a significantly smaller increase in sales price as opposed to the .0 to .5 increases(on average 14.8 % increase for .5 to .0 increase while .0 to .5 had a 26.2% increase on average for all homes with 3 bathrooms or less). This difference may suggest how adding a .5 bath to your home could give you a significant increase in the sales price while adding a shower to your .5 bath may not produce the same return on investment.
